# Design 10's bookmarks and cube picker critique (2026-10-08, T406)

**What this covers.** AC-343 and AC-329: 2026-09-27 design 10's Bookmarks (state B, with C, the sharing dialog) and its cube picker (state D), together with Filter on's grouped picker, which reuses the cube picker (T633, R64).

**What it leaves out.** Design 10's Jobs (main screen, pins 1–6) and Schedules (state A). 2026-10-01 design 16, approved in R56, supersedes both, and T611–T613 build it (AC-399).

**Not findings.**
- State D's dimmed "hidden by …" rows: R71 retired nearest-first resolution, and AC-342 with it.
- State D's "your copy of orders.yml" path: R72 and R73 (AC-428) replaced it with the "Your copy" chip, which is built.

**How it was judged.** Captures of the running app at 1,440 × 900 with sign-in off:
- the chart editor's "Cube or view" picker, opened;
- a dashboard filter's Filter on picker in the edit sidebar;
- the dashboard's Bookmarks, with none saved.

Bookmarks with rows, and with sign-in on, weren't captured. Those parts are judged from the source.

**Summary.** 11 findings (2 major, 4 minor, 5 polish), plus one already covered and one documentation note. Seven questions for the user.

## Questions for the user
1. **The Bookmarks footer (D10-04).**
   - (a) "＋ Bookmark this view" as an accent link, as drawn.
   - (b) Keep "Save bookmark…", recorded as a decision.
   - (c) "Bookmark this view…".
2. **Which date a bookmark shows (D10-05).**
   - (a) When it was first saved, the date the list is ordered by (R61).
   - (b) When it was last changed.
   - (c) None, recorded as a decision.
3. **An icon on the Bookmarks button (D10-06).** Design 10 draws one; designs 04 and 06 don't.
   - (a) Design 10's icon.
   - (b) No icon.
4. **"…" on items that open a dialog (D10-10).**
   - (a) Everywhere, as built.
   - (b) Design 10's words, with no "…".
5. **An empty Bookmarks popover.** The design is silent.
   - (a) As built: "No bookmarks yet", then the footer.
   - (b) A "Mine" heading over it.
6. **Field rows in Filter on.** The design is silent: state D draws cubes only.
   - (a) As built: the member's title, with "Cube · folder" at the right.
   - (b) The member's short title ("Name"), with "Customers · Shared" at the right.
7. **A picker for an item whose folder isn't stored (D10-01).**
   - (a) Take it as on the Shared root's path, and group it under "Shared".
   - (b) Make every saved chart and dashboard carry its folder, keeping the flat list only for a Cube that xcube doesn't serve.
