# Decisions: the compact dashboard header (lane HEADER)

Later decisions for P2.25 (F43, design 19), in the table of [`decisions-2026-10-08.md`](./decisions-2026-10-08.md)'s "Later decisions", kept here so the parallel lanes don't conflict there. T8xx-1, T828-1 and T828-2 are in that file.

| ID | Question | Decision | Why | Departs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T829-1 the row while editing and below 1,024 px (AC-484, AC-491, AC-495) | AC-484's one 48 px row is for view mode at 1,024 px and wider. What does the row do while editing, and in a narrow window, before T836 and T842 build them? | **It may still wrap there, at least 48 px tall, as the header did before.** View mode at 1,024 px and wider never wraps: the title, the tabs and the way back give way instead. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | The edit bar and "+ Page tab" don't all fit one row at every width yet, and a row that can't wrap would push the page sideways (AC-304). | No |
| T829-2 the tabs' underline and the row's rule (design 19 pin 3) | Design 19 draws the selected tab's 2 px underline over the row's 1 px rule (`margin-bottom: -1px`). Built so, the strip, which clips, cut both the underline and a focused tab's inset ring. | **The underline sits on top of the rule, not over it: 1 px higher than drawn.** Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | A 1 px difference, within R77's 4 px; a whole focus ring (AC-176) outranks it. | Yes, by 1 px |
| T831-1 the toggle's words at the edges (AC-486) | What are the description and tooltip with filters but none set, how is a filter made from a marking on two fields named, and what counts toward "m"? | **"0 of m filters set", with no names. A marking's filter is named by its fields' titles joined with "and" ("Country and Category"). "m" counts the filters a reader is shown — those on a field — and the filters made from a marking.** "Details" has no description: the sheet it opens says the rest. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | AC-486's form "n of m filters set: ‹their names›" taken literally at n = 0; a filter with no field yet isn't in a reader's bar. | No |
| T833-1 motion where the system can't say (AC-488) | AC-488 animates the sheet unless reduced motion is asked for. What happens where `matchMedia` is missing, or the system reports neither preference? | **The sheet moves only where `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` matches. Anywhere else it opens and closes at once.** The CSS's own durations are 0 s under `reduce` too, so the two never disagree in a browser. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | No motion is the safe side of WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.3. Every browser wechart supports has `matchMedia`; this also keeps jsdom's unit tests at rest. | No |
| T833-3 the sheet as edit mode comes and goes (AC-488, AC-491) | Built first, entering edit mode slid the sheet open over 200 ms, inert, so a pill pressed right after Edit was out of reach (keyboard-coverage:227 and :700 caught it). Does edit mode animate the sheet? | **No. Edit, Discard and Save show or hide the sheet at once, with the rest of edit mode's chrome (the edit bar and the sidebar, which don't move either). The 200/150 ms motion is the toggle's.** Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | AC-488's motion is design 19 pin 8's slide down from the toggle; edit mode switches the whole page at once, and an author's next press must land. | No |
| T834-1 what counts as "open first" for Escape, and Escape in the header with the sheet closed (AC-489, AC-119) | Which open things take Escape ahead of the sheet, and what does Escape do with focus in the compact row while the sheet is closed? | **The same list the dashboard's own Escape already waits on: a modal dialog, any menu, a filter's popover, the Bookmarks popover. With the sheet closed, an Escape in the row is the dashboard's, which clears the cross-filters as before.** The sheet marks the Escape it takes as handled (`preventDefault`), and the dashboard's Escape leaves a handled one alone. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | AC-489's order names the popover, the chip menu, Bookmarks and ⋯, which that list covers. AC-119's "the first Escape closes the sheet and clears nothing" applies only to the open sheet. | No |
| T836-1 the row while editing (AC-491; design 19 state C; supersedes T829-1 for edit mode) | Design 19 draws edit mode's row at 48 px. The row held a fixed 48 px in view mode, but why Save is held back is a sentence, which can need a second line. Does edit mode's row ever grow? | **It is one row and never wraps: "Editing ‹name›", the tabs scrolling in their strip, "+ Page tab", the edit bar and the gear, 48 px. Its height is its content's, not a fixed 48 px, so the held-back sentence may take a second line and make it taller rather than run out of it.** Below 1,024 px it still wraps until T842. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | Rule 114 and state C ask for one row with the sheet under it; a reason cut off or drawn over the tiles would break T114's "a disabled control with an explanation". | No |
| T837-1 measuring what fits, and naming "More ▾" (AC-492) | How is the room for the inline tabs found, what happens when even the open page beside "More ▾" doesn't fit, and what is "More ▾" called for assistive technology? | **The room is the row's width inside its padding, less every other part, the gaps, and the title's 160 px floor, since the title gives way before the tabs. Each tab's width is kept from when it was last drawn inline. It is placed again before paint on any change in the row's size, its parts' sizes or its parts. A pixel is kept back for rounding. Placed to fit, the strip doesn't shrink (`flex-shrink: 0`), so the title alone gives way. Where even the open page beside "More ▾" doesn't fit, the strip shrinks and scrolls as before rather than push the row's end out of the window. "More ▾" is a menu button named "More pages", which holds its visible "More" (WCAG 2.5.3), so it isn't confused with the actions' ⋯, named "More".** Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | Flex shrinks by weight times width, so with the strip at 1 it gave up a fraction of a pixel while the title still had room, and scrolled by one pixel. In the e2e at 1,024 px, the open page still fits whole beside "More ▾". | No |
| T838-1 the compact chips' parts (AC-200, AC-119) | What is a compact chip's accessible name, what does a cross-filter's compact chip menu hold, what is "+n", where does focus go after a ×, and which gives way first for room: the chips or the tabs? | **Each compact chip is a `group` named, and titled, by its words and its tile: "‹marking›: ‹values›, from ‹tile›" or "‹member› = ‹value›, from ‹tile›". Its menu is headed "‹marking›, from ‹tile›" (Copy link with marks, Unmark), or for a cross-filter its words and tile (Clear). "+n" is text; it says "n more: ‹names›" to assistive technology and in its tooltip, and the sheet's bar shows the rest whole. After a ×, focus goes to the next compact chip's ×, or else to the Filters toggle. The chips keep their width (values cut at 240 px), and page tabs that don't fit go behind "More ▾" (T837).** Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | AC-200 and AC-119 name the tooltip, the accessible name and the menu heading, and design 19 draws the cross chip without a menu, so the menu holds the full chip's one action. A hidden sheet's × can't take focus. Design D1 keeps both the chip and "More". | No |
| T839-1 the change highlight's edges (AC-493; T8xx-1) | T839's wording puts the changed state on the count, and T8xx-1 on the toggle. What happens on a second change within 2 s, or when the sheet opens during it? | **The toggle takes it (`.header-toggle--changed`, design 19's `.tog.hl`), as T8xx-1 decides. A second change starts the 2 s again. Opening the sheet ends it, since the bar then shows the change itself. A toggle shown anew, after edit mode for instance, starts unlit.** Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | The approved design and T8xx-1 outrank the task's shorthand. The highlight points to a change made out of sight. | No |
| T833-2 a move that never runs or is cut short (AC-488) | The sheet is inert while it moves, and the move ends on the height's `transitionend`. What if no transition runs (the height is already where it's going) or one is cancelled with none after it? | **It comes to rest 100 ms after its own duration (300 ms opening, 250 ms closing) unless a height transition has started; once one runs, its `transitionend` ends the move, however slowly the page plays it.** A pill's own transition ending is ignored. Lane HEADER's call, overturnable. | The sheet must never stay inert. Waiting on `transitionrun` lets the e2e slow the page's animations tenfold (DevTools' `Animation.setPlaybackRate`) to look at a move. | No |
