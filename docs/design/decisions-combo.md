# Decisions: combo charts (lane COMBO)

Later decisions for P2.26's combo charts (F44, design 20), in the table of [`decisions-2026-10-08.md`](./decisions-2026-10-08.md)'s "Later decisions", kept here so the parallel lanes don't conflict there.

| T845-1 where a combo series' mark and axis are kept (AC-497) | T845 names the sparse `options.series` and `options.combo` shapes. Where do the mark and axis live, and what goes in `options.combo`? | **In `options.series[‹measure›]`, as `mark` and `axis`, beside the colour and `hidden` that Customize › Series already keeps there (`SeriesOptions` in `render-spec.ts`). `options.combo` isn't added until the first chart-wide combo setting is stored (Synchronize, T849; stacking, T851), so no key exists that nothing sets.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | One map per series keeps the three places that set a mark (AC-497) reading one record, and keeps the settings inert on any other chart type, as `options` is meant to be. | No |
| T845-2 what Auto is, and what pins (AC-498) | Is a mark or axis pinned as a pair, and does a choice that equals Auto's pin? | **Each is pinned on its own: a chosen mark with the axis still Auto stays on the left, and the other way round. Any stored value is the author's and is kept, even one equal to what Auto would give, so a measure moved later keeps it. Auto goes by the measure's place in the Metrics well; a choice goes by the measure's name. Back to Auto (`withComboAuto`) drops both, keeps the colour, and leaves no empty entry.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | AC-498 keeps "the chosen mark and axis" through field changes, and the series row offers the two as separate groups. | No |
| T846-1 how a line's halo is drawn (§7h "Line over bar") | ECharts has no outline for a line. How is the 5 px surface halo drawn without a second legend entry, tooltip row or marked share? | **A second line series per line, in `bg-surface`, 5 px, with the line's values only, named as its line and listed after every series, at `z` 3: under every line (4), over every bar (2). It is silent, out of the tooltip (`tooltip.show: false`) and the legend's hover link, and it's added by `withLineHalos` after Customize, marking and highlight have drawn the option, so none of them sees it. An area's edge has none.** The layers' `z`: area 1, bar 2, halo 3, line 4, point 5 (`COMBO_Z`). Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | `research/echarts-combo-layers.md`: the legend takes an entry from the first series of a name, and the axis tooltip skips a series whose `tooltip.show` is false. A halo under every line, not just its own, also keeps a crossing line whole. | No |
| T846-2 two axes before T847–T849 (AC-497, AC-499) | With a series on the right, what does each axis show until alignment and titles land, and which measures does R67's "on another scale" leave out? | **Two value axes, left and right, each written in its own first measure's format (and whole-number ticks when it holds counts alone); the axis settings (`applyAxes`) still format a single axis only. R67's rule holds per axis: each measure is drawn if it shares its axis's first measure's scale (`undrawnOf`'s `axisOf`). A combo split by colour draws every series with its first measure's mark and axis until T852.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | A measure the author put on the right is drawn there (AC-497), and its ticks must read in its own unit; the split across every measure is T852's. | No |
| T846-3 an axis no series is on (T846's review) | With every drawn series on the right (or a split combo whose first measure is there), the left axis has nothing to read. Drop it, or keep it? | **Keep it, aligned, with its gridlines (the right's ticks sit on them) but no numbers and no axis line.** A split combo draws every series on its first measure's axis. Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | Rule 118 keeps gridlines on the left only; with no left axis there'd be none. Numbers for an axis nothing reads would invite a wrong reading. | No |
| T848-1 the glyphs as drawn (AC-500, §7h) | ECharts draws an axis title's glyph as a rich-text box and a legend's as a path, each with one fill and one border for all edges. How are the four glyphs drawn? | **Bar: an 8 × 8 box, radius 2. Line: a 12 × 2 box. Point: an 8 px dot with a 1 px surface ring in a title; in the legend, an 8 px dot (the legend's icons carry no stroke, T683). Area: a 12 × 8 block in the series' colour, its top edge being the block's own.** Titles read "‹glyph› Revenue · ‹glyph› Target ($)": measures joined by " · ", then the axis's unit in brackets: a currency's symbol, "%", or nothing for a plain number. Each title starts at the left axis's line and ends at the right's. Split by colour, a title's glyphs are `text-secondary`, since each stands for every member. A title set in Customize replaces a single axis's. Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | A 0.24 tint with a solid top edge needs two fills, which neither a rich box nor a legend path has. The block keeps the area's 12 × 8 size and its colour. | Yes: the area's tint and top edge |
| T848-2 what emphasises an axis (AC-500) | Hovering a series or its legend entry emphasises its axis. ECharts' legend hover dispatches `highlight`, but wechart's legend has `selectedMode: false`, which makes every entry silent. Is the legend made reachable? | **A hovered series emphasises its axis now: its title and ticks go to `text-primary` and the other axis's marks fade to 0.28 of their strength (areas from their 0.24). This is merged into the drawn chart and put back as the pointer leaves. A mark with its own strength (a marking's or a highlight's) fades from its own. Only a combo of two axes, never a trellis's panels. The legend's `highlight` isn't listened for until an entry is reachable by the pointer: T857's glyph buttons in the editor, or a later change to the legend. AC-500 stays open for that.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | Turning `selectedMode` on would also turn on legend toggling, which AC-070, AC-071 and the marking modes own. That isn't T848's to change. | No |
| T847-1 how two axes are aligned (AC-499, rule 118) | `alignTicks` doesn't align zero, so wechart computes the extents. How many ticks, from which steps, and what does "five ticks each" mean for −38K…155K beside −4 %…38 %? | **wechart fixes each axis's `min`, `max` and `interval` (`alignedAxes`). Steps are 1, 2, 2.5 or 5 × 10ⁿ (whole numbers only on an axis of counts). Where both ranges include zero, both axes get `n` intervals with `k` below zero, `n` within two of ECharts' `splitNumber` of 5 and `k`, `n` chosen to leave the least empty height across both. Otherwise the left takes its own nice extent and the right as many nice intervals. "Five ticks each" is read as ECharts' five intervals, six tick marks: −50K…200K by 50K beside −10 %…40 % by 10 %.** A combo's right axis keeps its settings in `options.axes.right`, beside the left's `options.axes.y`; include zero is the only one read so far (Min, Max and Log are T849, T850 and T858's). Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | No −38K…155K extent has five tick marks at a nice step (50K stops at 150K; 100K wastes half the height); five intervals is ECharts' own default target. | No |
| T845-3 a stored mark or axis the combo doesn't have (AC-497) | What happens to a chart stored with a fifth mark or a third axis? | **`readChartDocument` refuses it with `comboProblems`' words, as it refuses a stored grain Cube doesn't have. Drawing, which never sees such a chart from storage, falls back to Auto rather than inventing a mark. Series settings that aren't a map hold no mark or axis and are refused by nothing (T845's review).** A removed measure keeps its settings in `options.series`, as its colour does; whether the editor clears them on removal is the editor tasks' (T855, T856). Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | The reader is strict by design (`chart-document.ts`): a chart read as something else is worse than an error. | No |
| T849-1 what Synchronize compares, and where it's stored (AC-501) | "Both axes share a number format": which measures' formats, and what does the reason say for a plain number? | **Each axis's first measure's format, by `scaleOf` (currency and its code, percent, or number), since each axis is written in that format (T846-2). The reason reads "‹left first› is in ‹unit› and ‹right first› in ‹unit›", a plain number's unit being "numbers". Unavailable while every series is on one axis ("Every series is on the right axis.", T849's review). Stored as `options.combo.synchronize: true` (the first chart-wide combo key, T845-1); a stored choice whose formats later differ is kept but not drawn (`isSynchronized`). `withSynchronize` turning it on clears `options.axes.right`'s `min` and `max` and keeps the rest; off removes the key. Synchronized, both axes take one nice extent over both axes' data, zero in if either needs it, the left's fixed ends kept.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | Design 20 Q12 (a) and state C. | No |
| T849-2 Customize's min and max on two axes (decision T846-2, AC-219) | With two axes drawn, `applyAxes` skips the `yAxis` array, so a Min or Max typed in Customize changed nothing. How are they applied with the alignment? | **In `series/combo.ts`, per axis: `options.axes.y` for the left and `options.axes.right` for the right. A fixed end ends its axis exactly as typed, replacing the data's end (zero or no zero, as on one axis). The alignment then keeps both axes' tick counts equal and, where some `n`, `k` meets the fixed ends, zero level; otherwise the left takes its own extent and the right as many intervals. A fixed axis's interval is whatever divides its span into the shared count, not a nice step. A minimum at or above its maximum is ignored. Log is T850's; title and format are T858's.** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | AC-219's failing state is a setting accepted and the drawing unchanged. | No |
| T850-1 how two log axes are drawn and what's said of the values left out (AC-502) | ECharts' `alignTicks` uses a linear stub for log axes (research §2). How are two log axes aligned, where are log settings stored, and what does the note say? | **Stored as `log` in `options.axes.y` and `options.axes.right`; `withLogScale` writes both at once; `logScaleState` reads either as both while two axes are drawn, and neither while any series draws bars (the first bar measure named). `withSettledLogScale` hands the drawing the settled values, so a stored log beside bars is never drawn. Two log axes are given whole decades from each one's lowest value above zero, both as many as the wider needs; synchronized, one extent over both. ECharts ticks a log axis a decade apart under ten decades (research/echarts-axis-legend-bars.md), so the ticks meet; the SVG test checks it. A fixed end above zero is kept as typed and may put ticks off the other axis's. Values at or below zero on a log axis are drawn as gaps, and a `tile-notes` line under the tile reads "‹n› values not drawn: ‹measure› was 0 in Jan, Feb and Mar, and a log scale has no zero." (measures joined by "; "; "was at or below 0" and "nothing at or below zero" once any is negative).** Lane COMBO's call, overturnable. | Design 20 state E and Q3 (a). | No |
| T852-1 Auto while split (AC-504, AC-498, rule 122) | Auto draws the first measure as bars and the rest as lines, but split, two measures with one mark can't be told apart. | T851-1 where stacking is kept, and what an axis reaches (AC-503) | Stacking is chart-wide.
