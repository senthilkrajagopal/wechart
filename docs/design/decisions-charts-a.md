| T957-1 100 % stacking (AC-584) | Where Stack lives and is stored; what "isn't summed" covers; when series fold; what a stored 100 % does once the measure isn't summed; and how the axis and tooltip are drawn | **`options.stack`, `'percent'` or nothing for Sum (`withStack`), read only on Stacked bar and Stacked area.
