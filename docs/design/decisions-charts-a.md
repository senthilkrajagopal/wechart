# Decisions: new chart types, lane A (lane CHARTS-A)

Later decisions for P2.27's new chart types (F49–F55, design 23), in the table of [`decisions-2026-10-08.md`](./decisions-2026-10-08.md)'s "Later decisions", kept here so the parallel lanes don't conflict there.

| ID | Question | Decision | Why | Departs |

| R81-A1 the lane's calls on R81-2 and R81-3 (AC-631, AC-632) | What the answers leave unsaid: how a dimension's browser bin marks, what Metrics may hold, how a duration combines with "First to last", and Unit's default | **A dimension's browser bin marks and cross-filters as a range on the dimension, as a model bin does; Metrics takes a summed measure only, each bin summing its grouped values' measure. A duration is evaluated per bar (per row with One row, per member with "First to last" from its earliest start); Unit has no default, and no bars draw until the author chooses one; an empty duration is an open bar and a negative one a reversed row (AC-603).** Recorded under R81 in `requirements-log.md`. Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | A bin of the dimension's own values is exact on the dimension, so a value list would mark less precisely than the data allows; summing is the only way to combine grouped values that stays exact; the user's "with a chosen unit" names no default. | No |
| T934-1 R81's ids, and the 100 % cards (AC-559, AC-584) | Which of R81's fifteen are chart types, and what are the 100 % cards? | **Eleven new `ChartType`s: `waterfall`, `histogram`, `bubble`, `parallel`, `bullet`, `sankey`, `funnel`, `timeline`, `calendar`, `icicle`, `graphicalTable`. The KPI, the box plot and the Map stay `kpi`, `boxplot` and `map`. 100 % stacked bar and area are gallery cards (`stackedBar100`, `stackedArea100`) that pin `stackedBar` or `stackedArea` and pass `stack: 'percent'` to the editor; until T957 adds Customize › Stack, the editor pins the stacked type and ignores the stacking.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | 23-Q15 (a) draws the 100 % cards as presets ("switching Stack back to Sum is the same chart"); AC-594 makes Bubble a card with its own needs and greying, and AC-560 a top pick, so it has an id; existing saved KPIs, box plots and country maps keep their ids (AC-562, AC-622). | No |
| T934-2 a greyed card's highlighted well (AC-217) | Which well is highlighted, and for how long? | **The first well the type lacks, in its requirements' order (`missingFor`), for today's types too (a Line with no time highlights Time); the well keeps `data-highlight="true"`, an accent outline, until the chart's fields next change or another type is drawn (by the gallery, Auto or a quick pick; the editor keeps the type it was chosen for and compares it). A requirement may name a type's own well (`target`), which `Wells` hands to the type's own wells module.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | AC-217 says "the editor then highlighting the well it needs"; a highlight that outlived the next edit would point at a well already filled. | No |
| T934-3 the Map card's words before its layers (AC-559, AC-622) | The Map card takes design 23's description and needs now, while only the Regions layer on Countries exists until T973 … T978 | **Its name ("Map"), description ("Regions, points or density on vector boundaries") and needs ("Needs a region key or a location, and a measure") are design 23's from T934; its greying stays today's (a dimension of ISO-3 codes, the Countries set's region key), so its missing sentence reads "Map needs a dimension of ISO-3 country codes in Dimensions; add 1 more." until T973 widens it.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | AC-559 names the card in design 23's words; an ISO-3 dimension is a region key of today's one boundary set, so the needs are true of what draws today. | No |

No card is named or described as a gauge.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | Design 23 screen 1 pin 7 asks for the two cards on that search; their descriptions don't hold the word. | No |
| T934-5 R81's types in the walks over every type (AC-037, AC-178) | `chart-editor.spec.ts`'s "no chart type is left undrawn" and `table-view.spec.ts` walk `CHART_TYPES`, which now holds types not drawn yet | **`e2e/r81Awaiting.ts` names R81's types still waiting for their series (`AWAITING_SERIES`) and their table view (`AWAITING_TABLE_VIEW`); the walks leave those out, and each type's series task and table-view task takes its line out. `optionFor.test.ts` likewise counts R81's types apart from the DOM types.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | Each R81 type's drawing and table view is its own task with its own criteria; the walks keep covering every type built, and each type joins them on the day it is built. | No |
| T935-1 which tiles say "share shown", and in what words (AC-098) | Every tile whose items are partly marked, or R81's share-showing types only? And what "‹marking summary›" reads as | **The graphical table, the waterfall and the histogram (AC-098's list; 100 % stacking joins with its Stack setting, T957), while its marked share is asked of Cube: the marking holds a member the tile doesn't group by (`needsMarkedShare`, AC-104's own test for "partly marked"). The badge reads "‹marking name›: ‹values›'s share shown", the name kept as every badge names its marking: one or two values by name ("EMEA", "EMEA and APAC"), more as "EMEA, APAC +2", a range by its bounds, items of several members or a whole tile marked counted as the badge counts them ("1 item's", "31 items'"). The wiring is in `DashboardView`'s `markingOn`, beside the badge's other facts.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | AC-098 names the R81 tiles; today's bars and KPIs already say "n marked" in tests and designs the user approved, which R81 didn't ask to change. | No |
| T936-1 where a KPI's settings live, and how its wells are drawn (AC-564) | T936 names `chart-spec.ts`; and design 23 sheet 1 draws the five wells but not their controls | **In the chart's options under `kpi`, sparse (`withKpiOptions`), as lane COMBO keeps a series' mark in `options.series` (T845-1): no `kpi` key while nothing is set, Direction written only when not Higher is better, so a KPI saved before R81 saves byte for byte as it was. `chart-spec.ts` (the editor's wells) is unchanged. A setting that can't be read is left out, not refused. Time holds the trend's time dimension and its grain (day to year), the period being that grain's (T937 asks Cube for it). Each well is a native `select` named by its label, Compare to's measure and value and Target's value appearing beside it as chosen, Direction a radio group; R81's type wells now sit right after Metrics (moved from after Time), and the KPI keeps the editor's own Dimensions and Time wells as every type does, its own Time well headed "Time period and trend", design 23 sheet 1's words, so it never reads as the query's Time well (the coordinator's call after T936, 2026-10-18). Time's period is the grain's current one ("this month"); a chosen other period (last month, a range) isn't offered, AC-564 naming only "the period and the trend's time dimension" — to raise with the coordinator if T937 needs it.** The wells show what is stored, holding only a pending choice (a measure, value or grain still to pick) themselves, so an undo shows what is left. Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | Options are where a chart's drawing settings already persist and round-trip through `readChartDocument`; native controls are keyboard-reachable with the keys R75 allows; AC-564 places the wells "after Metrics". | No |
| T943-1 a graphical table's columns, and their settings before lane TEXT's (AC-570) | Where the columns live, how a column is "R80's element settings for that kind" while lane TEXT's settings forms (T884–T886) aren't built, and which of R80's settings a column inside a chart takes | **In the chart's options under `graphicalTable`, sparse (`withColumns`), as `{ columns: [{ kind, …attrs }] }`, each column R80's element of its kind stored as the Text tile stores it (Bullet as `bulletGraph`), and read back only where the Text tile's own schema (`textDocumentProblems`) accepts it; a column it refuses is left out, the rest kept in order (as T936-1). Rows and Group rows by are the chart's first and second dimensions, as parallel coordinates read theirs. A column's settings open below its row (`editor/wells/graphicalTable.tsx`), design 21 C1–C5's fields for its kind: Measure; a Change's Over, Compare with (the previous period only, as R80 stores it), Period, Show as and Direction; a Sparkline's Over, By, Show the last and Mark the last point; a Bullet's Target (Fixed or a measure), Ranges (up to 3, low to high, a fourth refused with "Up to 3 ranges, low to high."), Min and Max; an Icon's As % of and its rules, top to bottom, each When, Value (a percentage while As % of is set, stored as R80's ratio), State and Label, + rule adding an Otherwise rule. A column takes no marking and no filter of its own: the table marks its rows as table tiles do and every column shares the chart's filters (AC-573). A new column starts on the chart's first measure. Remove shows the app's Undo toast ("Removed the column ‹name›."), and is also one step of the editor's Undo. When lane TEXT's settings forms land, these should become them, as one component; until then they write the same attributes. AC-570 stays open until T944 draws Group rows by's sections.** Lane CHARTS-A's call, overturnable. | Design 23 sheet 2: "Each column's settings are R80's element settings (design 21, state C) for that kind"; storing R80's own attributes, checked by R80's own schema, keeps a column from ever holding a setting R80's element couldn't; a per-column marking would contradict the rows' own marking. | No |
