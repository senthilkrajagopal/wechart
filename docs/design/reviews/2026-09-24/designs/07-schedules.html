What changes, and why

A platform operator comes here to answer "when does this rebuild, and who decides?". The page answers it for every pre-aggregation at once, and routes every change to where it is made: Model for the schedule, Jobs for builds. Wording follows AC-153/AC-154 and the refresh-key research.
