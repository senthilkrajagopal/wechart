What changes, and why

A business reader opens this page to read four numbers and two trends. Today the KPIs are 14 px body text, every chart title competes with a timestamp to the second and a "Table view" button, and the time chart is 240 hair-thin bars of height 1 on a 0.2-step axis.

  1. 1
    Description and the Markings button in the headerThe dashboard's description (already stored, Settings › General) under its title, and direction §7a's Markings button, whose panel lists each marking with its summary. Preview as and Schedule stay out: they are undecided.UX-027
  2. 2
    Filter band unchangedIt already matches the prototype (53 px, scope tags, hint on the right). Shown with filters so the tile heads can be judged in context.—
  3. 3
    KPIs in the KPI ramptext-kpi 32/40/600 with tabular figures in a row (direction §3), text-kpi-solo 40/52 when a KPI stands alone, the label above in the tile-title style, the value in the member's format ($597.2K). Today: 14/400 body text.UX-020 · UX-041
  4. 4
    The title comes first; one small badge says where the data came from"✓ Pre-agg · 3m" (status-coloured, as the prototype's cache badge) or "● Live". The time to the second moves into the badge's tooltip. Nothing else sits in the head at rest, so the 688 px tile shows its whole title. Today: "● Live · as of 11:12:06 PM" plus a "Table view" button, and the title truncates once marked.UX-021
  5. 5
    Actions on hover and focus only; Table view lives in ⋯↻ ✎ ⋯ appear when the pointer or keyboard focus is on the tile (26 px, as today). "View as table" is already the menu's item, so the separate button goes. The menu is state B below.UX-021 · UX-029
  6. 6
    Time reads as a line, counts on whole numbersAt month grain, three 2 px lines with end-point labels (≤ 4 series, direction §6) and ticks 0, 20, 40, 60 — never 0.2 for a count (minInterval: 1 for count and count_distinct). The recommender prefers a line once a time axis has more buckets than bars can show; direction §6's own example is "Time × 1 measure, 184 dense points → line". Proposal — needs the user's approval choosing a coarser default grain when none was chosen.UX-022
  7. 7
    Table tiles use the data gridThe same grid as the editor's drawer: 12 px, 28 px header band, 24 px rows, right-aligned tabular amounts, and a row count in the head. Today tile tables are browser-default and repeat the time column.UX-024 · UX-023

The marked-rows actions the spec asks for (AC-113, AC-114) show disabled until they land, so the menu's shape doesn't change under readers. "View as table" replaces the header button.
