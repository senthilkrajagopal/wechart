Dashboard settings — a page with a title, a primary Save, and choices that explain themselves

The settings page as the prototype draws it (dash-settings.js): a title bar with Back, Cancel and a primary Save; a 220 px nav with Active / Inactive tags; flat content. The Interactions section, the most consequential choice on a dashboard, gets radio cards that say what each mode does and how readers use it. Markings' colour slots use the prototype's swatch buttons instead of Chrome-blue radios.

Interactions

How readers explore this dashboard by clicking its charts. One mode is active at a time; the other mode's settings are kept, not cleared.

Markings

Readers mark items in a named, coloured marking. Every tile that uses the marking highlights them, on every page tab, and details tiles can show only what is marked.

What changes, and why

Measured today (app-26, app-27): no page title; Back flush at x 0 and Save flush at x 1440 with no bar padding or rule; Save settings is a secondary button; the nav is 280 px with 36 px grey-selected rows; content sits in a white card; mode choices are one-line boxes with no explanation; Markings uses native Chrome-blue radios; Esc does nothing.
