What changes, and why

The layout, order and component rules are the prototype's; where this design goes beyond the prototype it says so. Every change keeps the app's existing behaviour behind it (history, AC-217's gallery, AC-029's trace, AC-216's grain and filters).

  1. 1
    Header reads like a page title, with the answer's facts as badgesBorderless title (its field appears on hover and focus), Raw query or ✓ Pre-aggregation, and 8 rows · 42 ms; the ownership note with ⚠ when more than one dashboard uses the chart; ↶ ↷ icon buttons. The rollup-only sentence moves into the Raw query badge's tooltip and the Acceleration tab instead of a yellow banner over every chart.UX-062
  2. 2
    Joined cubes in the field listEvery cube reachable from orders through its joins is a group marked "via join" (collapsed after the first). Country comes from Customers, and now you can see where. Fields carry the 18 px role chip, their aggregation or distinct count, and a 6 px accent dot when used. One name per field: "Total Amount" here and on the pill.UX-060 · UX-061
  3. 3
    Cube or view at the top of the inspectorAs in the prototype. It also stays named in the field pane's head so the list says whose fields they are.UX-050
  4. 4
    Chart type card with quick picksIcon, name and "Chosen manually · Change…" (Change opens the existing gallery). Under it, Auto plus the recommender's top pick and runners-up as 24 px icon buttons, the drawn one pressed. Auto replaces the separate "Back to recommended" button and is hidden while already in Auto. Each icon's tooltip carries its runner-up reason.UX-051 · UX-065
  5. 5
    A Recommended card only when your pick differs, with a reason from the data"8 countries × 1 measure → bars, sorted by value": the signature and cardinalities the recommender already computes, in ≤ 80 characters (AC-028), and a Use button. Replaces the fixed per-type sentence and the "Or" bullet list. Goes beyond the app's current recommender strings; the data is already there.UX-052
  6. 6
    Compact dashed wells, role-tinted pills12/600 label above a 36 px dashed well (radius 6); pills 24 px, radius 4, blue for dimensions and orange for measures, the aggregation as a tag that opens a small menu (sum, avg… where the model allows). The four wells now take 256 px instead of ~480.UX-053 · UX-056
  7. 7
    "+ metric" opens the picker, not a native selectA 264 px menu with a filter box, measures with orange Σ chips grouped by cube ("via join" for the others), each with its aggregation; members already used are shown checked and disabled. Fully keyboard-driven (↑ ↓ Enter Esc), which is also checklist rule 11's route. See state A below.UX-054 · UX-096
  8. 8
    Sort beside Top N, directly under the wellsThe control that changes the chart most is no longer at the bottom of the tab. The app's richer choices stay (label A→Z / Z→A).UX-055
  9. 9
    "Why this recommendation?" last, as a readable checklistCollapsed by default. Open, it lists the signature, each field's distinct values and only the rules that decided, in sentences with ✓ / ⚠ icons; "Show every rule for every type" keeps AC-029's full table one click away. See state C.UX-059
  10. 10
    The preview sits in a tile, under a one-line statusSame frame as on a dashboard (1 px border-subtle, radius 6, 16 px padding), so what you see is what the tile will be. The status line names the drawn form and what a click does.UX-063
  11. 11
    Results drawer open by defaultTabs always visible; a formatted grid (Design 02 has every tab and state).UX-058
