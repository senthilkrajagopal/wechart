Compact dashboard header: one 48 px row by default, with the rest a slide-down that pushes the tiles

The user, 2026-10-17: "nearly 30% of the page goes away for the dashboard header and the filters. Can we have a more compressed header that can be expanded with a slide down so that the complete header can be seen... default will be the compressed header." Measured from today's CSS, the chrome above the tiles is 274 px: 30.4 % of a 1,440 × 900 window and 38.1 % of 1,280 × 720 (app bar 48, header 128, page tabs 45, filter bar 53).

This design draws:

  • The compact header, the default: one 48 px row holding the way back, the title, the page tabs, a summary of what's marked or cross-filtered, the actions, and a Filters toggle that shows how many filters are set. With the app bar, the chrome is 96 px (10.7 % at 900, 13.3 % at 720), 178 px less than today.
  • The expanded header: the toggle slides down a sheet with the description, the ownership line and today's filter bar, unchanged. It pushes the tiles down and never covers them.
  • States: the heights; a filter changed while the header is collapsed; edit mode; narrow widths; no filters; many filters; a long title; no description; and the keyboard and motion.

Screens 1 and 2 are Wren Writer, a read-write member of Sales / EMEA, reading the shared dashboard "Revenue overview" in Markings mode, with two products marked and three of four filters set. Sign-in is on.

What doesn't change: the app bar; the My workspace band and the proposal band above the header (rule 72); the filter bar's pills, popovers, scope tags and chips (AC-077, AC-200); the actions and their order (design 04 state E, design 13); the tiles and the canvas.

A reader opens a dashboard to read its tiles. Today a third of a laptop window is title, tabs and filters before the first number. The compact row keeps on screen what a reader acts on (where it lives, which page, what's narrowing it, and what they can do) and folds what they read once: the description, the ownership line and each filter's pill.

The sheet holds what the compact row folded. Its filter bar is today's, pill for pill; only where it sits changes. It pushes the tiles down rather than covering them, so the KPIs a filter changes first stay in view while it's being changed (Q1).

Keyboard and accessibility

R75: no new shortcut. The toggle is an ordinary button reached with Tab, pressed with Enter or Space, as the WAI-ARIA disclosure pattern defines. Esc closes the sheet only because rule 85 already makes Esc close every panel; it isn't part of the disclosure pattern itself.

MomentKeysWhat happens
Focus order, collapsedTabThe way back → the page tabs (one stop; ← → along them, as built) → More, when shown → each compact chip's menu and × → History → Bookmarks → the primary → the gear → ⋯ → the toggle → the canvas. The title is a heading, not a stop.
The toggleEnter · SpaceA button with aria-expanded and aria-controls naming the sheet. Its name is "Filters, 3 set" ("Details" with no filters); its description is the tooltip's text (B3). Focus stays on it when it opens, and the next Tab goes into the sheet: the full title link, if shown, then each pill, the chips, and on to the canvas.
The sheet—A region named "Filters and details", directly after the row in the DOM. Closed, it is hidden; moving, inert. The filter bar inside keeps its own section aria-label="Filters" and every key it has today.
Esc, in orderEscInnermost first. (1) An open filter popover, chip menu, Bookmarks popover or ⋯ closes, focus back on its control, as today. (2) Otherwise, with focus anywhere in the header (the row or the sheet) and the sheet open, the sheet closes and focus moves to the toggle. (3) Otherwise, today's dashboard-wide Esc: unmark all, or clear the cross-filters (AC-119). Esc on a focused tile still unmarks that tile only (AC-094) and never closes the sheet.
Closing with focus insideEnter on the toggle · EscFocus never stays inside a hidden sheet: it goes to the toggle.
Announcements—Opening and closing are conveyed by aria-expanded alone, with no live text. A filter, marking, cross-filter or bookmark change while collapsed is said once, politely, through the dashboard's existing live region (B2, B4, B5), and not again by the toggle.
Below 1,024 pxEnter · ↑ ↓"Page: Overview" is a menu button whose items are the pages, the current one checked; History and Bookmarks are menu items in ⋯.
Reduced motion—@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): no transition. The 2 s highlight stays, since it doesn't move.
