Combo chart: each series its own mark and axis, two aligned value axes, one split across every measure, and reference lines and bands

The user, 2026-10-18: "let us work on combo charts and rich text charts... make it modern.. use the best technologies available." R80's answers, followed exactly here: 2 value axes, left and right; one split dimension across all measures, so series = measure × member, capped at 8; and this round's extras: area as a series mark, log scale per axis, reference lines and bands, and a Synchronize axes toggle. Both experts agreed, and R80 takes as given: each series picks its own mark and axis; a second axis is never automatic and, when drawn, its zero and ticks align; AC-218 warns only while more than one unsynchronized value axis is drawn.

This design draws:

  • Customize › Series: one row per series with its mark (Bar, Line, Area, Point) and its axis (Left, Right), and Auto. The same two choices sit in the Metrics pill's menu and, in the chart editor, behind each legend entry's glyph.
  • Two value axes drawn zero- and tick-aligned, each titled with its measures and their mark glyphs; Synchronize axes; log scale per axis, matching on both while two are drawn; the Right axis group in Customize › Axes.
  • The split: one dimension across every measure; colour follows the member, the mark tells the measures apart; at most 8 series, with "not drawn" pills beyond.
  • Reference lines and bands, horizontal and vertical, each labelled where it's drawn.
  • The AC-218 warning, only with two unsynchronized axes, offering One axis, Synchronize, Small multiples and Table.
  • States: the legend's menu, Synchronize on, the warning's variants, log scale, the reference forms, limits, the axis groups, below 1,024 px, and the table view.

Screens 1 and 2 are Wren Writer, a read-write member of Sales / EMEA, editing the dashboard-local chart "Revenue and margin by month" in My workspace, from the sample model's orders cube.

What doesn't change: the editor's shell, header, field list, results drawer and Data tab layout (design 01, 2026-09-24); the type card, quick picks and Recommended card; the recommender, which still never proposes two value axes (AC-030, rule 8); the palette and its order (§5); marking and cross-filtering on combo marks (§7a); the tile frame on dashboards.

Screen 1: what's new, and why

A combo is a set of layers over one category axis. Each measure is its own series, with its own mark and its own side; nothing about a second axis happens unless the author asks for it. Once two axes are drawn, wechart makes them as honest as two axes can be (zero and ticks aligned, titled by glyph) and says plainly, to the author only, what they can still imply.

  1. 1
    The canvas line says what's drawn"3 series on 2 value axes, aligned at zero". With one axis it reads "3 series on one value axis". The type card on the Data tab says "Combo · Chosen manually" as today, and carries the warning's icon while two unsynchronized axes are drawn (state D).AC-217 · AC-218 (amended)

A radio group each, so arrows move along it. "3 of 8" counts drawn series against the limit.AC-219 (extended) · references-combo §5 · Spotfire A2
  3. 3
    Auto, per rowA new combo draws the first measure as bars and the rest as lines, all on the left (today's combo.ts; Sigma's default). A row the author changed shows ↺, "Back to Auto: line, left"; a manual mark or axis stays pinned through later field changes (principle 2). Auto never puts a series on the right.rule 8 · principle 2 · AC-030
  4. 4
    Bars: Side by side or StackedOne setting for every bar series in the chart, as Spotfire's Appearance page has it; stacks never cross axes (each axis stacks its own bars). Areas get the same row, Overlap or Stacked, while any series is an area (Q11).AC-219 "whether series stack" · Spotfire A4

Screen 2: the split, and how marks and axes are set from the Data tab

R80: "One dimension, all measures". The split is set once for the chart, and every measure is drawn for every member: series = measure × member. Colour then means the member, as it does in every other wechart tile (rule 7), so the mark has to tell the measures apart.

  1. 1
    The type card names the risk onceWhile two unsynchronized axes are drawn, the card adds the warning's icon and "Two scales, not synchronized · See Axes", which opens Customize at the warning. The full warning lives in one place (screen 1 pin 6).AC-218 (amended)
  2. 2
    Metric pills show mark and axisEach pill starts with its mark's glyph and ends with an L or R tag before ×. Its menu (open here on Margin %) holds Draw as (Bar, Line, Area, Point) and Axis (Left, Right) as radio groups, then Back to Auto, Format… and Remove. The inspector stays the complete keyboard path; the menu is a shortcut (principle 5).references-combo §5 · Looker, Metabase, Sigma
  3. 3
    One mark per measure, while splitWith a split, two measures with the same mark couldn't be told apart, because colour already means the region. So a mark another measure uses is shown disabled, naming that measure ("used by Revenue"). Unsplit, any two series may share a mark (Q1).rule 122 (new) · Q1
  4. 4
    "not drawn · over 8 series"A third measure would make 12 series. Its pill takes the "not drawn" form R67 introduced, with the reason, and the row under the well offers Small multiples by Region (one combo per region, every measure drawn), Table, or Remove. Nothing is dropped silently.R67 · R69 · rule 6 · rule 117 (new)
  5. 5
    The Split by wellOne dimension, across every measure (R80). A second dimension dropped here replaces the first (the well's replace rule); Shift-drop is refused with "A combo splits by one dimension; use Small multiples for a second".   6. 6

  8. 8

Keyboard and accessibility

No new shortcut (R75). Everything here is a button, a radio group, a switch or a menu, with the keys those already have. The inspector is the complete path; the pill menu and the legend glyph are shortcuts to the same choices.
