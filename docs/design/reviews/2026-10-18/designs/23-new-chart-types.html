New chart types: fifteen components, each with its gallery card, wells, a drawn example, marking and cross-filtering, its recommender rule, its empty and over-limit states, and its text alternative and table view

R81, the user, 2026-10-18, about the study references-new-chart-components.md: take up all fifteen ("Top tier (1–5), Spotfire analysis (6–8), Mix & flow (9–12), Time & hierarchy (13–15)"); proceed without inventorying the .dxp files; no gauge; and design them in parallel with R80's combo and rich tiles. So the KPI and the graphical table draw R80's sparkline (64 × 16, design 21 pin 9), Change (▲/▼, sign, a status text role) and bullet graph (three greys, a text-primary value bar, a 2 px target) exactly as designs 20 and 21 do.

This design draws:

  • Screen 1, the chart-type gallery as built (AC-217), with the new cards on their purpose shelves, what each needs, and a proposed ninth shelf, Flow.
  • Screen 2, a dashboard in Markings mode using four of the new types together (the KPI tiled by Region, a graphical table, a waterfall, a bullet chart and a histogram) with EMEA marked from the KPI, so each draws the marked share its own way.
  • Fifteen type sheets, grouped by gallery purpose. Each holds the gallery card, the wells, a drawn example on sample data, how it marks and cross-filters, when Auto proposes it (if ever) with the reason it would show, its empty and over-limit states, and its text alternative and table view.
  • Keyboard and accessibility, every new state as an icon and a label, the criteria this changes, and 19 questions.

The screens show Wren Writer, a read-write member of Sales / EMEA, on the sample model's orders cube. Measures such as Revenue target, Margin target, Return rate and the funnel's stage counts, the batches in the timeline, the sales territories and the store locations are illustrative.

What doesn't change: the editor's shell, wells and Customize tab (design 01, 2026-09-24; design 20); the gallery's dialog, search and "Use Auto" (AC-217); the recommender's existing rules and caps (AC-028 … AC-031) except where a sheet says so; §5's palettes; §7a's marking gestures and rendering; the 12 px floor and the 400 and 600 weights; and the keyboard as built (AC-176, R75). No type here needs a second value axis (AC-030).

Keyboard and accessibility

No new shortcut (R75). Every new control is a well with its "+" picker, a radio group, a switch, a select or a menu, with the keys those already have. Marking items from the keyboard on the new types is new keyboard reach, which AC-176 holds for your approval: it is drawn as 23-Q8 (a), reusing §7a's existing keys, and nothing of it is built until you approve. Until then, and always, the table view (AC-178) is the complete path to every value.
