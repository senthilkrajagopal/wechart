My workspace in Explore — a private place with its origin and target on every item

Today My workspace is the last root in the tree, with a generic ◉. It has no band, and the same empty-state line as any folder ("Use ＋ New to add a dashboard or chart"). Its New menu has no Model. Shared folders offer Dashboard and Chart in New, and a shared dashboard's primary action is Edit, in place (UX-233, UX-234, UX-235, UX-241). This design follows references.md surfaces 3 and 5 and rules 68–74. The main screen is a read-write member's workspace with the New menu open.

What changes, and why

The workspace becomes a place you can tell apart at a glance: first in the tree, banded, and listing where each thing came from and where it's going. Creating asks where it's going first (AC-282), with a picker that can't be misread.
