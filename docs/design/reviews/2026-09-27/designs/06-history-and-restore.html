What changes, and why

History lives beside the item, like Access. It reads as a list of who changed what and who let it in, because in wechart every version was approved by someone, sometimes its own author.

  1. 1
    History in the header of a shared itemClock icon and label, pressed while the panel is open. Only for read-write members and Super-Admins (decision 5). A read-only user never sees it (AC-289).AC-289 · decision 5 · rule 68
  2. 2
    A 360 px panel, docked rightIt takes focus on open, closes on Esc and returns focus to History (rule 85). It replaces Access if that's open. URL: ?panel=history, so a reload keeps it.surface 7 · AC-002
  3. 3
    The current version is taggedA "Current" chip on the newest row. The header's version line says the same.rule 80
  4. 4
    Author and approver on every row"by author · approved by approver", or "by author · self-approved" when they're the same person, because self-approval is allowed and should be visible.AC-289 · R17, R18
  5. 5
    Descriptions clamped to two lines, with More"No description" when there was none. A restore's description says what it restored (version 6).surface 7
  6. 6
    Row actions on hover and focusView draws that version in the centre, read-only (state A). For models and data sources, Compare with current shows the diff (states B, D).surface 7
  7. 7
    Propose restoring…, never RestoreOpens Design 05's Propose dialog, titled "Propose restoring version 7 to Sales / EMEA", with the description filled in (state C).AC-289 · rule 80
  8. 8
    The limit, said once"Only the last 10 versions are kept", so a missing version 0 isn't a surprise.AC-289
