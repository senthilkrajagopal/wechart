Sign-in, the account, and what each role's top bar and Explore show

The account button and menu are built and good: initials and name, the identity, a Super-Admin badge, Sign out (AC-252). Around them, several things are missing. The switcher still lists Model, and lists every workspace to every role, a reader included (AC-001, UX-231). There's no Reviews control. Signing out drops straight into the provider's form rather than wechart's "You've signed out" state (UX-281). A user with no access sees a root named "Shared" with ＋ New and "+ Folder" (UX-230, UX-232). This design covers references.md surface 1 and rules 59–61, 68.
