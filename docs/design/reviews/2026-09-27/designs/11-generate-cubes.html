Generate cubes — design 01's docked pane, in three steps: Source, Tables, Review

You asked for the Model workspace, New cube and Generate cubes to be brought in line with the rest of the app. Today Generate cubes is a centred 960 px modal that grows at every step. Its top moves from y 367 to y 32, its height from 167 to 836 px, and its primary from y ≈502 to y ≈836, until everything scrolls inside it (critique MW-20). It covers the tree and the model, which are exactly what you need to see to avoid a clash. A clash is a plain bullet that reads like a column left out, and the reason Publish is pale sits below the fold (MW-21). The controls are 14/400 labels, native checkboxes in the browser's blue and 75 × 32 "Columns" buttons (MW-22). The column preview doesn't say which table it shows (MW-23). The YAML falls back to ui-monospace, white on white, and scrolls inside the scrolling dialog (MW-24). The foot has three buttons and there is no ✕ (MW-25).

This design follows the critique's "Generate cubes: recommended layout" in the frame of the new data source wizard (design 01): a 480 px pane docked beside the model, at a fixed height, with a step rail, a context strip, and Back · Cancel · one primary pinned at the bottom. The API flow, the one publish of every file, the clash rule and every message stay as built. Generate still publishes straight to the model (your decision D4). The table under the notes checks every function against today's, and lists what the critique's layout adds.

What changes, and why

The flow, its rules and its words stay as built. What changes is the frame, the order and the density. The frame is stable and doesn't cover the model. The steps say where you are. The table list can be scanned, and each table's columns open where you asked for them. Each generated file has a status, and a clash says how to clear it. The YAML is the model's own code view.
