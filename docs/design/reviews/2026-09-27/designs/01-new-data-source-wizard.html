New data source — the wizard in a docked pane, with every form grouped by what it asks

You said the wizard looks bad. The review measured why (UX-201 … UX-214). The dialog changes size and position at every step and for every driver, so Next moves between y 592 and y 836. Long forms scroll inside it. The eight drivers are identical text buttons. TLS switches sit between the database and the sign-in. Ways to sign in hide in a native select. The Test step shows nothing until it runs, and then shows prose.

This design keeps the as-built flow and its rule. The flow is Type → Connection → Test → Name and target, and a data source is made only from a passing test of exactly its settings, with no override (AC-158, your answer of 2026-09-27). The wizard moves into the approved prototype's docked right pane (admin-sources.js), at a fixed height, with the steps at the top and the actions pinned at the bottom.

Addresses UX-201 … UX-214, UX-220, UX-221 · AC-158, AC-159, AC-160, AC-282, AC-325 · rules 3, 23, 35, 74, 83, 85 · Driver facts: packages/core/src/data-sources/drivers.ts and docs/research/xcube-slice-6.md. Host, user and the failure text are the review's real test against superset-postgresql (wrong password).

What changes, and why

The wizard's steps and its gate stay as built. Its frame, grouping and wording change: one stable frame, a type choice you can recognise and compare, forms that read top to bottom as where → how it signs in → TLS, a test you can see before, during and after it runs, and nothing lost by a stray Esc.
