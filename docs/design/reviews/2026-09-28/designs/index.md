# Designs for approval — UI design 2026-09-28 (phase 2)

One static mockup for R43, exporting and importing a dashboard (F38, AC-354 … AC-359). It is numbered after the ten phase-2 designs and designs 11 and 12 in `../../2026-09-27/designs/index.md`.

- **Revised 2026-09-28:** the Import half is redrawn to the user's simplified workflow (AC-356 … AC-358 as amended):
  - An import always lands in My workspace as new drafts, then follows the usual flow: a target, then Propose, then Approve.
  - Before Import, the pane warns about what the drafts may affect, and never blocks.
  - The one exception: a data source whose name the target already holds must be mapped to that one, or created under another name (AC-282, "Map or create").
  - With sign-in off, the import also goes to My workspace, and the single user self-approves.
  - The Clashes step (Replace · Keep both · Skip) is gone.

  Export is unchanged.
- **Format:** the same as the 2026-09-27 designs: a self-contained HTML file with inline CSS in direction.md's tokens, and Inter and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts, with no other requests. Open it in a browser. It shows two 1440 × 900 screens with numbered pins, Export and then Import, each followed by the notes on its pins and its other states. Then come the keyboard and accessibility rules, and the proposals and questions.
- **Where it comes from:** R43 and F38 in `docs/spec.md`, and the approved designs it builds on:
  - 01: the docked pane, and its Test step;
  - 03: the secret-field wording, and Test to save;
  - 04: My workspace, the target picker and the row menus;
  - 05: the Propose dialog, its checks, and proposals.

  Evidence on other products comes from `docs/research/`: `grafana-panel-schema.md`, `superset-chart-serialization.md` and `metabase-card-serialization.md`.
- **Illustrative content:** people, folders, dashboards, versions and numbers are illustrative. Screen 2 imports into a second wechart, whose content differs from the first's on purpose. Driver fields come from `packages/core/src/data-sources/drivers.ts`, and chart types from `packages/core/src/render-spec.ts`. The failed test's text is design 03's.
- **Build note:** importing models needs T347 (workspace models), and bundling them with the dashboard's proposal needs T350. Model rows are tagged "Build: needs T347". State V shows the import with models switched off until then; export offers models already.
- **Proposals:** anything not in R43 or AC-354 … AC-359 is marked **proposal — needs the user's approval**, in the mockup and below.

## Questions for you

Each is drawn one way so the design is complete; the way drawn is only a default until you decide.

- **Q4. The file's format and extension.** R43 says only "one file".
  - Drawn: JSON, named `revenue-overview.wechart.json` after the dashboard, and editable before export.
- **Q5. The "Not offered" count.** Rule 67 says nothing reveals an item you may not see.
  - Drawn: a count with its reason, with no names or folders, as design 05 counts breaks in folders you can't see (AC-322).
  - The alternative is to say nothing.
Settled, answered or moot:
- Q1 is settled by the user's "Map or create": there's no Replace for a data source. A name the target already holds is mapped to that one or created under another name (AC-282, state P).
- Q7 is settled: with sign-in off, the import goes to My workspace too, and the single user self-approves (R19, AC-254, design 09; state U).
- Q6 (dependencies start ticked) is answered: ticked.
- Q2 (Skip for a library chart) and Q3 (Replace with sign-in off, and History) are moot, since an import never replaces or skips.
