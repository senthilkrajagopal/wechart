| T992-1 the violin's density and width (AC-592) | AC-592 and design 23 give the violin's colour, opacity, edge and the box's width, but no kernel, bandwidth, width scale, tails or degenerate case. | **wechart's own `custom` series (not `@echarts-x/custom-violin`, research `echarts-violin.md`).
