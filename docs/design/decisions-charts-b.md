| T981-2 "one format" and Shared's reason (AC-597) | When do measures share a format, and what does the disabled Shared say? | **Two measures share a format when the model gives them the same one: a format name (`currency`, `percent`), a described format's name (`currency_0`), or none ("plain numbers"). A stored Shared that the formats refuse reads, and is drawn, as Each axis.** Lane CHARTS-B's call, overturnable. | AC-597 names no reason's words; naming the formats says what to change. | T991-1 how points are placed, from a fixed seed (AC-592, AC-306) | ECharts' jitter is unseeded and gives up on overlaps without a word (research `echarts-scatter-jitter.md`). How are All and Beeswarm placed? | **wechart places every point itself and hands ECharts a pixel `symbolOffset` per point. All: each category's points jittered across half its band, by a generator seeded from the category's name (mulberry32 over its FNV-1a hash), so a category's points don't move when another changes. Beeswarm: from the least value up, each point at the place nearest its category's centre that keeps it 8.5 px from every point placed before it, measured against a plot no larger than the one ECharts lays out (the canvas less the grid, the value labels and the category labels as `fitCategoryLabels` may turn them), on a value axis whose ends wechart sets to the values' least and greatest rounded out to a step of about a fifth. A category whose swarm would leave its band is drawn as All. While Points is All or Beeswarm the value axis has those set ends; Outliers keeps its axis as before. The tooltip says "beyond the whiskers" only of a point that is. Labels are reckoned turned as `fitCategoryLabels` turns them, by the narrower of its span and the band (T991's review). Known limit, accepted: y-axis settings stored under `options.axes` by an earlier type (a box plot has no Axes panel) still override the set ends and can bring a swarm's points closer.** Lane CHARTS-B's call, overturnable. | "A fixed seed" and "no two overlap" are then true by construction and testable on the drawn SVG. Positions depend only on the data and the canvas, so a redraw or an export of the same size places every point the same. | No |
