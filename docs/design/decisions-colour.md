# Decisions: categorical colour (lane COLOUR1)

Later decisions about how series are coloured, in the table of [`decisions-2026-10-08.md`](./decisions-2026-10-08.md)'s "Later decisions". They are kept here so the parallel lanes don't conflict there.

| ID | Question | Decision | Why | Departs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COLOUR-1 a tile never draws a member grey while it fits the palette; 16 categorical slots (AC-039, AC-099, AC-598) | The user's screenshots of the deployed Sales dashboard show the combo "Revenue and orders by month" drawing its Revenue bars and its Orders line in the neutral `#b3b1ab`, and the parallel coordinates "Category profile" drawing every line in it. The user: "all these have to be fixed.. with the coloring", then "expand to more colors then.. use 16 or 32 whatever makes sense..". The dashboard's assignment claims members in document order, and the earlier tiles had taken all 8 slots, so every later member, those measures included, got the neutral. Parallel coordinates without Colour drew every line in the first measure's colour, which was the neutral. | **(1) The categorical palette has 16 slots in both themes** (`CATEGORICAL` in `packages/ui/src/tokens.ts`; direction.md §5). Slots 1–8 are unchanged, so nothing already drawn changes colour. Slots 9–16 are cyan, ochre, cobalt, wine, lavender (an indigo in dark), raspberry, olive and purple, picked by a research agent from a survey of published palettes at the user's request ("use visualization agent and online documentation on what the 16 color palette can be"). They are validated as a set with slots 1–8 (`docs/research/palette-validation.md`, 2026-10-19), and AC-099's audit covers all 16. The neutral applies only past slot 16. The coordinator chose 16 because 32 can't be told apart. **(2) A per-tile fallback in `dashboardColours`.** The dashboard keeps its colour wherever a slot is free. A tile that colours no more members than the palette has slots never draws one in the neutral: a member whose dashboard colour is the neutral takes, on that tile only, the first slot no other member of the tile has. The dashboard's `assigned` state is unchanged, so the member keeps its place once a slot frees up. A colour the tile's Customize tab chose stays, even the neutral's hex. "Other (n)" is not a member and stays neutral. A tile of more than 16 coloured members keeps the neutral past slot 16. A Text tile's sparkline is drawn in the colour its measure has on the first chart that draws it, and otherwise never in the neutral either. **(3) Parallel coordinates without Colour and with 16 or fewer lines colour each line by its member** (Line by's value): the dashboard's colour for that member, or, standing alone, its place in the palette. That colour goes in each data item's own `lineStyle.color` (research/echarts-parallel.md, "A line's own colour"). They claim their lines' members, not their measures, which are axes. With more than 16 lines, every line is the palette's first colour, and nothing is claimed. With Colour set, nothing changes. The lines' names stay in text ink. **(4) 100 % stacking folds past the palette's 16 slots** (`SHARE_SERIES_CAP`), not 8. **(5) Unchanged:** the combo's 8-series cap (AC-505, R80, which is the user's own cap rather than the palette's; the coordinator, 2026-10-19), and the recommender's 8 colour categories (AC-031, which decides what to recommend, not how to draw). The box plot, the bullet, the graphical table and the KPI tiles were checked and never drew the neutral: a box plot is a single series or colours its categories by palette position, a bullet is drawn in greys and ink, and the graphical table and KPI take their measure's colour or the palette's first. | A neutral says "Other" or "no data" (direction.md §5). Drawing a tile's own two measures in it says they are the same, and that neither is a real series. That is false and unreadable. Keeping the assignment as it was keeps AC-039's promise wherever it can be kept, and limits the departure to tiles that would otherwise be grey. | AC-039's "the same colour in every tile" now yields, on a dashboard of more than 16 members, to "never grey on a tile that fits the palette". AC-039, AC-099 and AC-598 are amended, as are direction.md §5 and rules 6 and 7. |
